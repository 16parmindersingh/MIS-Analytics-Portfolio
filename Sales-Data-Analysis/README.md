# Sales Data Analysis Dashboard 📊💰

![Dashboard Screenshot](dashboard.png)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes 4 years (2014-2017) of U.S. superstore sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to create MIS reports and actionable insights for optimizing category performance, regional strategies, and customer engagement. By transforming raw CSV data into interactive pivot tables and a dynamic dashboard with slicers and charts, I addressed business questions on sales trends, profit margins, and geographic distribution.

This project showcases my skills in **advanced Excel**, **data analysis**, and **management reporting**, making it a valuable addition to my portfolio for roles in MIS, business intelligence, or data analytics.

---

## Dataset Description
The dataset consists of a single CSV file, representing sales data over 4 years from 2014 to 2017:

- **salesdata.csv**: Contains detailed order information.
  - Columns: `Order Date`, `Customer Name`, `State`, `Category`, `Sub-Category`, `Product Name`, `Sales`, `Quantity`, `Profit`.
  - Sample Data: 9,994 orders (e.g., 03-01-2014, Darren Powers, Texas, Office Supplies, Paper, "Message Book...", $16.45, 2, $5.55).
  - Categories: Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology.
  - Sub-Categories: Chairs, Phones, Binders, etc.
  - Data Insight: Covers 50 U.S. states with varying sales and profit figures, enabling comprehensive regional and temporal analysis.

## Results and Insights
### Overall Sales Performance
- **Total Sales Growth**: Sales data spans 2014-2017, showing consistent growth in customer engagement, with unique customer counts rising from 595 in 2014 to 693 in 2017 (a 16.5% increase). This indicates expanding market reach over the years.
- **Monthly Sales Patterns**: Sales peak in the later months of the year, with September ($307,650), November ($352,461), and December ($325,294) accounting for over 51.6% of total sales ($2,296,920). This suggests seasonal trends, recommending targeted promotions in Q3-Q4.
- **Annual Customer Trends**: Customer acquisition grew steadily, with 2016 seeing the highest unique customers at 637, correlating with increased sales volumes in high-profit categories.

### Category and Product Insights
- **Top Revenue Generators**: The Technology category leads overall, with Phones generating the highest sales at $330,007, followed by Chairs ($328,168) in Furniture and Storage ($223,844) in Office Supplies. This highlights the dominance of high-value items.
- **Profit Trends by Category**: Technology showed the strongest profit growth, rising from $21,493 in 2014 to $50,685 in 2017 (a 136% increase), while Furniture profits declined from $5,470 to $3,018 annually. Office Supplies averaged ~$30,623 per year, suggesting a focus on Technology.
- **Low-Performing Categories**: Fasteners and Labels contributed the least to sales ($3,024 and $12,486 respectively), indicating potential areas for optimization.

### Geographic and Customer Insights
- **Regional Sales Distribution**: California dominates with $457,688 in sales (over 20% of total), followed by New York ($224,732) and Texas ($170,188). Underperforming states like Wyoming ($1,603) and West Virginia ($1,210) suggest expansion opportunities.
- **Top Customers by Profit**: Tamara Chand tops the list with $8,981 in profit contribution, followed by Raymond Buch ($6,976) and Sanjit Chand ($5,757). The top 5 customers generated over $32,782 in profit, emphasizing key account nurturing.
- **Customer Profit Concentration**: Bottom performers (e.g., Cindy Stewart at -$6,626) indicate potential losses, recommending customer segmentation review.

### Business Implications
- **Category Optimization**: Focus on Technology (e.g., Phones) for profit growth and reduce emphasis on low-sales items like Fasteners.
- **Regional Strategy**: Target underperforming states with tailored marketing.
- **Management Recommendations**: Use seasonal insights to plan inventory and promotions.
